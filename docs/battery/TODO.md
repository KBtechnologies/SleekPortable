    - [18650](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/18650_battery)
  - [LiFePO4 cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_iron_phosphate_battery) are to be prefered
    - [longer lifecycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_iron_phosphate_battery#Better_aging_and_cycle-life_characteristics) - both in charge/discharge cycles and time!
    - [safer cell chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_iron_phosphate_battery#Safety) - not prone to thermal runaway!
    - only ~15% less capacity compared to [lithium-cobalt-nickel cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium-ion_battery)
    - with cell voltage of 2,5V - 3,45V a 2S1P or even 2S2P pack makes the most sense...
      - A matching [BMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battery_management_system) is needed and should be integrated into the pack or case.
